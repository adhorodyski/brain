What the checkmarks add up to

Forty-three rows; six exist nowhere at Kody. The identical marks cluster in Memory, Access and Running-it — the same idea at different scale, which is the honest admission that this is their free 15%.
  • Six rows exist nowhere at Kody: fill percentage, refusal-on-overflow, the same refusal covering patch, a shared two-person brain, per-note ownership, and no model key on the worker. The shared brain is the one thing their FAQ says they will not build. A seventh — the block appended to every tool response — they do partially, via an overlay hosts are known to drop.
  • Fifteen rows where Kody does more, each with its reason on the row — and the largest by far is runs code the agent wrote, which is also the biggest line item on their bill.
  • Three rows are genuinely undecided: reminders, curation, and whether "free" survives our own usage. All three are cheap to try and cheap to drop.
  • The tool surface is seven, and that number is the design. ask would put a model turn on the request path; web_fetch is not memory and would carry the fence, the host allowlist, the fat-write risk and the page-injection path with it. Hosts fetch their own pages.
